# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

## Introduction

Welcome to Still.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Still ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

By using Still, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information We Collect

Still is designed to collect as little personal information as possible.

### Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

* Favorite quotes
* Theme preferences
* App settings
* Notification preferences
* Premium purchase status

This information is stored locally and is not transmitted to our servers.

---

## Purchases

Premium purchases are processed through:

* Google Play Store
* Apple App Store

We use RevenueCat to manage premium access and purchase validation.

We do not receive or store your payment information.

For more information, please review:

* RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
* Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
* Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy

## Advertising

AdMob may collect certain device information and usage data to provide and measure advertisements.

## Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Still may send local notifications on your device.

Notification settings can be modified or disabled at any time through your device settings or within the App.
